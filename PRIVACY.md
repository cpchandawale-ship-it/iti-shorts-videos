# ITI Shorts Uploader – Privacy Policy

ITI Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the ITI NIMI Channel (Researcher Chandpasha) to upload his own educational YouTube Shorts to his own channel.

- It only uploads videos and sets their titles, descriptions and tags on the owner's YouTube channel.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of anyone else.
- The YouTube access token is stored only on the owner's private server and is used only for this purpose.
- Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
